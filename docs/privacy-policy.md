We will only process special-category information where we have both a lawful basis and an applicable additional condition under the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018. Depending on the circumstances, this may include explicit consent, provision of services requested by you, or the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

We will only collect and use information that is relevant to the service, limit access to those who need it, and keep it securely. We will not use coaching information for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects.
